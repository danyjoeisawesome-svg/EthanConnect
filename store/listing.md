## Full description
EthanConnect turns your Android TV into a wireless second screen for your Windows PC.

* Share your PC screen - or a real extended second display - on the TV over your own Wi-Fi.
* Control the PC pointer with the TV remote: D-pad moves the mouse, OK clicks, hold OK to right-click, channel keys scroll.
* Use your phone as a trackpad and keyboard: scan a QR code from the PC, no phone app needed.
* Private by design: end-to-end encrypted on your local network, pairing PIN, no accounts, no cloud, no ads.
* Works as a lightweight TV launcher too, with an input switcher and a shortcut back to your TV's original home screen.

**Requires the free EthanConnect PC app** (Windows 10/11) from https://github.com/danyjoeisawesome-svg/EthanConnect/releases. Install it, then open "Second Screen" on the TV and enter the PIN shown on the PC.

## Release notes (1.0.0)
First release: PC second-screen streaming, PIN pairing, remote-as-mouse, automatic reconnection when the PC's IP changes, TV input switcher.

## App access instructions (for Google review - required because the app needs a PC)
The app streams from a companion Windows app. To test: (1) install EthanConnect PC from the releases page above on a Windows PC on the same Wi-Fi/LAN as the TV or emulator; (2) in the TV app open "Second Screen" > "Pair with PC"; (3) enter the address and 6-digit PIN shown in the PC window. No account or login is needed.
